# Portfolio City

A side-scrolling comic-book city that presents Bunthoeun Tok's professional profile as a game, alongside a plain Résumé view for visitors who don't want to play.

## People

**Visitor**:
Anyone viewing the site; recruiters are the visitor we design for first.
_Avoid_: User, player

## The game

**Hero**:
The original spider-themed character that represents Bunthoeun in the city.
_Avoid_: Spider-Man, Spiderman, player character

**City**:
The single continuous street the Hero travels along, made up of Districts.
_Avoid_: Level, world, map

**District**:
One stretch of the City devoted to a single section of the profile (Intro, About, Experience, Projects, Contact).
_Avoid_: Scene, page, screen, zone

**District map**:
The overview that lets a Visitor jump the Hero straight to any District.
_Avoid_: Menu, fast-travel, navigation

**Anchor**:
A point above the street (rooftop corner, sign) the Hero's web can attach to while swinging.
_Avoid_: Grapple point, hook

**Landmark**:
A thing in a District that stands for one profile item, such as a job's building or a project's billboard; each belongs to exactly one District.
_Avoid_: Card, object, sign

**Panel**:
The comic-styled overlay that shows a Landmark's full details when the Visitor interacts with it.
_Avoid_: Modal, popup, dialog, card

**Skill token**:
A collectible representing one of Bunthoeun's skills, placed somewhere in the City.
_Avoid_: Coin, item, pickup

**Secret rooftop**:
The hidden place unlocked once every Skill token has been collected.
_Avoid_: Easter egg, bonus level

## Outside the game

**Résumé view**:
The plain, scrollable presentation of the same profile content, reachable from anywhere in one tap.
_Avoid_: CV page, classic mode, fallback
